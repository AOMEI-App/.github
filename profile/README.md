## 🚀 Introduction
AOMEI Partition Assistant for Windows is a complete storage toolkit that reaches well beyond basic drive formatting. It is built for people who want to shape their disks deliberately — reallocating space, restructuring layouts, and moving systems between drives without starting from scratch. The software pairs a free Standard edition for everyday work with a Professional edition that unlocks deeper operations. For technicians and demanding users, AOMEI Partition Assistant Professional turns intricate disk maintenance into a series of clear, controlled steps.

## ✨ Highlights

### Core Partitioning
- **Resize, move, merge, and split** — Reshape partitions to match how your storage is actually used.
- **Format and label volumes** — Prepare new or repurposed partitions quickly and consistently.
- **Convert disk styles** — Move between MBR and GPT or between primary and logical partitions.

### Advanced Operations
- **Migrate OS to another drive** — Transfer your Windows installation to a new SSD or HDD with the AOMEI Partition Assistant Pro toolset.
- **Allocate free space and align partitions** — Redistribute room between volumes and align partitions for better SSD performance.

1. Start AOMEI Partition Assistant and study the disk overview it presents.
2. Choose a disk or partition and open the operation you need, such as migrate, convert, or resize.
3. Configure the details in the wizard, confirming targets and sizes as you go.
4. Use **Apply** to commit the queued operations and track their progress to completion.

## ❓ Common Questions
**Is AOMEI Partition Assistant free, and what does Professional add?**
The Standard edition is free and covers everyday partitioning. AOMEI Partition Assistant Professional is the paid edition that adds advanced capabilities such as system migration and additional conversion and allocation tools.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
The software supports current 64-bit Windows desktop editions, including Windows 10 and Windows 11. Running the newest version keeps it aligned with recent Windows updates.

**Is my data secure during advanced operations?**
Operations such as migration and resizing are designed to protect your existing data as they execute. For major changes, keeping a current backup remains the safest practice.
